A simpler way to reuse

How EcoSwap could work.

Four easy steps illustrate the intended experience of a future local reuse marketplace.

1

Explore local listings

Browse categories, compare example prices and search by suburb to find what you need.

2

Check item details

Review photos, condition, dimensions and suggested collection location before enquiring.

3

Arrange a safe exchange

In a future live platform, users would confirm details and agree on a safe public meeting place.

4

Give items another life

Reuse, share or donate items and help make local consumption more circular.

Good habits

Tips for safer second-hand exchanges

Verify product condition and details, avoid sharing sensitive information, meet in public where practical and never send money to someone you do not trust.

For large items, plan transport and bring help if needed.
