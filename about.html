Our story

Reuse is a community effort.

EcoSwap Adelaide is a proposed digital marketplace that makes reusing everyday goods feel easier, more affordable and more local.

The idea

Why we created this concept

Many households have items that are still useful but no longer needed. Meanwhile, students, renters and families often need affordable furniture, clothing and home essentials.

EcoSwap Adelaide explores how a simple online platform could bring these needs together and encourage a more circular way of consuming.

🌏

Our mission

Make reuse an easy first choice for people living in Adelaide.

Our vision

A connected local community where useful products stay in circulation for longer.

This website is a university assessment prototype, not an operating marketplace.
